# Repair strategy (R)

Code the strategy used to repair the episode. A response may sequence multiple strategies, but select the primary strategy for the present turn.

R1. open_request
Group: Initiation (R1-R3): opens repair by requesting clarification or offering a candidate for evaluation.
Definition: Signals possible misunderstanding but leaves the trouble source unspecified.

R2. restricted_request
Group: Initiation (R1-R3): opens repair by requesting clarification or offering a candidate for evaluation.
Definition: Requests clarification of a specific missing or ambiguous component.

R3. restricted_offer
Group: Initiation (R1-R3): opens repair by requesting clarification or offering a candidate for evaluation.
Definition: Offers a new bounded candidate interpretation or action and requests evaluation or confirmation.
Do not use when: Only checking uptake of an established repair; use R7.

R4. refashioning
Group: Resolution (R4-R6): supplies a repaired formulation, suggestion, or added detail.
Definition: Replaces the problematic referring expression or description with a newly formulated one.

R5. suggestion
Group: Resolution (R4-R6): supplies a repaired formulation, suggestion, or added detail.
Definition: Proposes a provisional candidate referent or action that the partner can accept, reject, or revise.
Do not use when: Giving a direct correction or elaborating an established description; use R4 or R6.

R6. elaboration
Group: Resolution (R4-R6): supplies a repaired formulation, suggestion, or added detail.
Definition: Adds discriminating information to the original description, such as a landmark, direction, constraint, or contextual detail.

R7. acceptance_check
Group: Closure (R7): checks acceptance or implementation of an already established repair.
Definition: Explicitly requests or inspects evidence that the partner accepted or implemented an already established repaired interpretation before continuing.
Do not use when: Introducing a new candidate interpretation or action; use R3.
