# How is possible misalignment detected? (H)

Code one primary observable cue that first makes possible misalignment warrant attention. A cue is evidence of possible non-grounding, not proof of a private mental state. "Work" means the task result a participant produces (for example, a drawing, a document, or a configuration); it can be observed directly or described by the participant who produced it.

H1. other_initiated_repair
Definition: The recipient explicitly signals a problem with the other party's prior contribution and requests clarification, specification, repetition, or confirmation of what it meant.
Use when: A participant produces an open request ("what?"), a restricted request ("which one?"), or a restricted offer, that is, a candidate understanding of the other party's contribution put up for confirmation ("you mean the left one?").
Do not use when: The participant only asks the other party to evaluate their own result or work ("like this?", "how is this?") without questioning what the prior contribution meant; that is not H1.
Operational rule: IF a message explicitly asks to clarify, repeat, or specify the other party's prior contribution, or offers a candidate understanding of it for confirmation, THEN classify H1.

H2. insufficient_acceptance_evidence
Definition: A contribution needing uptake receives no positive evidence of understanding after a real opportunity to respond.
Use when: No acknowledgement, relevant next contribution, restatement, or consistent task action follows the contribution.
Do not use when: The partner has not yet had a real opportunity to respond, the delay comes from the interface, the contribution needs no uptake (for example, a closing acknowledgement), or the content is already settled between the parties.
Operational rule: IF a contribution that needs the partner's uptake (an instruction, proposal, question, or repair) has had a real opportunity for a response, AND no later message or observable action shows acknowledgement, an accurate restatement, or a task action consistent with it, THEN classify H2. A real opportunity exists when the partner's next contribution addresses something else, or when enough time has passed with no observable partner action.

H3. incompatible_public_response
Definition: A public reply, restatement, or action demonstrates an interpretation incompatible with an earlier shared instruction, task state, or commitment.
Use when: The incompatibility is observable in the dialogue or in a shared representation.
Do not use when: It is only a guess about intention.
Operational rule: IF an earlier instruction, task state, or commitment conflicts with a later reply, restatement, or action, AND the conflict can be stated as an observable expected-versus-observed difference, THEN classify H3.

H4. action_plan_deviation
Definition: A task action, observed directly or described by the partner, departs from an agreed or externally represented plan, procedure, route, or constraint.
Use when: There is a traceable reference plan and a later action that does not fit it.
Do not use when: An action is merely unsuccessful and no plan was established; the action is still in progress toward the plan (for example, a partial result, or an undone step being redone); or the difference is imprecision that would not change the outcome. An action that fits the plan but not the latest instruction about it is not a departure: the instruction was wrong.
Operational rule: IF the partner's new work, observed directly or as they describe it, departs from the active part of the plan in a way that would change the outcome or where the next step must start, THEN classify H4. Establish the active part from the plan itself, and use the latest instruction only to locate which part is being worked on. A part of the plan that the work has already completed or passed is no longer active.

H5. workspace_representation_conflict
Definition: The parties' workspaces differ on a task-relevant object, location, version, visibility, or state.
Use when: The difference can be tied to the shared map, document, canvas, or system view.
Do not use when: The difference exists but nothing in the current exchange depends on it.
Operational rule: IF the two parties' workspaces differ in a way that a current contribution depends on (what an object is, where it is, or whether it can be seen), THEN classify H5.

H6. self_reported_uncertainty
Definition: A participant explicitly states uncertainty about their own understanding, interpretation, or ability to proceed.
Use when: The uncertainty is directly expressed.
Do not use when: Treating uncertainty alone as proof that the two parties are misaligned.
Operational rule: IF a message directly states uncertainty about how to interpret or continue the task, THEN classify H6. Do not infer H6 from behavior alone.

H7. not_observed
Definition: No observable cue for H1–H6 is present.
Operational rule: IF none of H1–H6's conditions is satisfied by the current evidence, THEN classify H7.
