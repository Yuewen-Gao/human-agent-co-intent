# Outcome (O)

O1. solution
Definition: The repair problem is solved with positive evidence that collaborators can proceed on the repaired interpretation.

O2. abandonment
Definition: The repair problem remains unsolved as interaction moves on, changes topic, or explicitly ends the attempt.

O3. explicit_disagreement
Definition: Collaborators understand each other's positions but retain different preferences, priorities, or decisions.

O4. not_observable
Definition: The available record lacks sufficient evidence to classify solution, abandonment, or explicit disagreement.
