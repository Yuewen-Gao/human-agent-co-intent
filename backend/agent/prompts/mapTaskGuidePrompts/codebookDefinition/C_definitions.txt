# Interpreted cause (C)

C1. contextual_information_not_communicated_or_retained
Definition: Relevant context, prior decisions, constraints, or conditions was not communicated, retained, or available when needed.

C2. unevenly_distributed_information
Definition: Collaborators possess different task-relevant information, representations, or access to information.

C3. information_salience_not_communicated_or_understood
Definition: Information is available but its importance for the current decision, task outcome, or constraint is not communicated or understood.

C4. unequal_speed_of_information_access
Definition: Collaborators obtain or inspect relevant information at different times or rates, plausibly contributing to the mismatch.

C5. silence_interpretation_ambiguity
Definition: Silence has multiple plausible meanings, leaving understanding, action, availability, or confusion indeterminate.

C6. problematic_reference
Definition: A referring expression does not identify one shared referent, object, place, version, or meaning in the current interaction.

C7. problematic_sequential_implicativeness
Definition: Collaborators interpret the interactional action of a contribution differently, such as instruction, revision, proposal, confirmation, or incidental remark.

C8. insufficient_evidence_to_infer_cause
Definition: The record shows a mismatch cue but lacks evidence to distinguish among other causes.
