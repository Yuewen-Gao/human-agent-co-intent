# Mental-model update relation (M)

M records an observable change in the explicit, task-relevant working interpretation. It does not claim access to private mental state.

M1. specified
Definition: A task-relevant proposition that was absent or underspecified becomes explicit.
Do not use when: An explicit, valid proposition only gains detail; use M2.

M2. elaborated
Definition: A still-valid working interpretation gains distinguishing detail without replacing prior content.
Do not use when: The prior interpretation is replaced by an incompatible one; use M3.

M3. refashioned
Definition: A problematic explicit formulation or representation is replaced by a revised formulation.
Do not use when: The earlier formulation remains valid and only gains detail; use M2.

M4. confirmed
Definition: An existing explicit interpretation receives positive evidence of sufficient acceptance or understanding for the current task.
Do not use when: A confirmation was only requested or a proposal merely presented; use M5.

M5. no_observable_update
Definition: The record does not show that an interpretation was specified, elaborated, refashioned, or confirmed.
