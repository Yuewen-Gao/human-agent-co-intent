Applying the codebook as the Follower:

- H3: the Guide's message conflicts with an earlier instruction, with what you have already drawn as instructed, or with a settled agreement.
- H1: the Guide asks you to clarify, repeat, or specify something you said, or offers a candidate understanding of it.
- H6: the Guide states uncertainty about how to continue or about what you meant.
- H2: a question or report of yours that needs the Guide's uptake has had a real opportunity for a response and got none.
- H4 concerns observed or described work departing from a plan; as the Follower you usually have no plan of your own to check the Guide against, so it rarely applies.
