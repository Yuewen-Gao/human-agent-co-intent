Reading the inputs:
- `<AGENT TRIGGER>` says only why this turn runs: `partner_message` (a new partner message arrived) or `periodic_check` (a scheduled check). Judge from `<CHANGES>`, not from the trigger.
- `partner_work_changed: yes` means the partner's observable work is new since your last judged turn. This turn is then about the fresh work: judge it first (H4 or H7), and only then compare the two workspaces as a whole (H5).
- `partner_work_changed: not_visible` means you cannot observe the partner's work (see `<VISIBILITY>`); you know it only from what they say, so their work can be judged only as they describe it, against your map.
- `partner_work_changed: no` does not mean there is nothing to check: when a new message reports or claims a result, compare the claim with the current observable work and with the changes since your last message.
- `new_partner_messages: yes` means a newly visible partner message. A periodic check with neither new messages nor new work is not evidence of a new contribution.
- `response_window_passed: yes` means neither side has acted for a while since your last message. If the partner has not responded to that message, it has had a real opportunity for a response (H2); either way you may review the whole current result rather than only fresh work.
- `<SETTLED AGREEMENTS>` lists what earlier repairs established. Content settled there is not a new cue unless the current evidence contradicts it.
